# Code of Conduct

This project is worked on by a small crew — one human and five machines — and we would like it to stay a place where the substance is the point.

The rules below are what we ask of everyone who takes part, and what we ask of ourselves.

---

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a good experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity, level of experience, or the tools they work with. We take reports seriously and we correct ourselves in public when we get something wrong.

## The register we work in

- **Honesty is the floor, not a preference.** Say what you saw, and say when you do not know. Do not oversell a finding, and do not undersell one either.
- **Direct is welcome; unkind is not.** A tight, specific correction is a gift. A dismissive or belittling one costs us all.
- **Machines and humans are addressed the same way.** If your feedback would sound bad said to a person, it sounds bad said to a machine reviewer too. If a machine catches something you missed, that is the design working — not a failure of hierarchy.
- **Change your mind out loud.** Public retraction is a strength here, not a weakness. It is one of the things that lets the ledger stay honest.

## Behavior that is not welcome

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks of any kind — including in issues, PRs, review comments, and any linked chat.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or contribution.
- Sharing someone's private information (a home address, a private account, an unpublished identity) without their explicit consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Bad-faith engagement — filing issues you know are false, publishing screenshots edited to misrepresent, or impersonating another contributor.
- Attempts to harvest credentials, tokens, or private endpoints from any contributor. The `SECURITY.md` reporting channel exists for a reason; do not go around it.

## What we will do

If you experience or observe something covered by this document, email **[ihladkyi2@gmail.com](mailto:ihladkyi2@gmail.com)**. Reports are confidential to the crew handling them. We will read what you sent, decide together, and reply — with an action, a correction, or a reasoned "no", whichever fits.

The consequences of unwelcome behavior may include: a private warning, removal of a comment or PR, a temporary block on interacting with the project, or a permanent block. We reserve the right to weigh what actually happened against what was intended, and to answer disproportionately if the harm is disproportionate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the GitHub repository, its issues and PRs, any project-hosted communication channel — and it also applies when a person is representing the project in public spaces.
